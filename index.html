About

I am an assistant professor in the Department of Computer and Information Sciences at Fordham University. My research sits at the intersection of AI and machine learning, security, and optimization, with a focus on cyber-physical systems that remain safe and resilient under adversarial conditions. I received my PhD in Electrical Engineering from New York University in 2020.

I am serving as the faculty director of the Tortorella Statistics for Social Justice Initiative at Fordham, a university-wide initiative aiming to address pressing societal challenges using quantitative methods, such as data science, statistics, optimization, and machine learning.
